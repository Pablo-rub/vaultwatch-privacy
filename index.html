VaultWatch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

VaultWatch ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the VaultWatch mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account information: email address and password when you register, or your Google account information if you sign in with Google.
  • Usage data: movies, TV shows, episodes, ratings, lists, and other content you add to your account within the app.
  • Device information: general device data collected by third-party services (see below).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app and your personal watch history.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure.
  • To display personalized content recommendations based on your watch history.
  • To process and manage your Premium subscription.

3. Third-Party Services

VaultWatch uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase – database and authentication provider. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – used to display ads to non-Premium users. Privacy Policy
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) – used to fetch movie and TV show information. Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention

Your data is stored as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the app's Settings screen, which will permanently remove all your data.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely via Supabase with row-level security policies ensuring each user can only access their own data.

6. Children's Privacy

VaultWatch is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings.
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your VaultWatch account and all associated data at any time by following these steps:

  • Open the VaultWatch app
  • Go to Profile → Settings
  • Scroll down and tap Delete account
  • Confirm the deletion

This will permanently delete your account and all data associated with it, including your watch history, ratings, lists, favorites, and subscription status. This action cannot be undone.

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us at anaklusmos.dev@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: anaklusmos.dev@gmail.com
